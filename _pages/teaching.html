Student laboratory projects

I supervise experimental projects within advanced courses of the BSc and MSc programmes in Physics. In the BSc programme (courses Complementi di Elettromagnetismo and Complementi di Introduzione alla Fisica Moderna), students carry out optics experiments in our lab. Recent projects include:

  • Temporal coherence and mode structure of laser sources. Fourier spectroscopy with a Michelson interferometer on a red diode laser and a He–Ne laser, to measure the coherence length, linewidth and longitudinal-mode structure.
  • Laser beam profiling. Characterization of the radius and waist of a Gaussian laser beam using the knife-edge method and a CCD camera, on a self-diffraction setup.
  • Surface plasmon resonance. Reflectance of a prism coated with a thin gold film: numerical modelling from Maxwell's equations and measurement of the plasmon resonance.

In the MSc programme the projects are more research-oriented: MSc students contribute to ongoing research projects in the laboratory.

Lectures

  • Solid-state sources of quantum light, EULiST Blended Intensive Programme "Principles of Quantum and Nonlinear Photonics", University of L'Aquila, 2026. Two lectures on the generation of quantum states of light in solid-state devices, from transition-metal dichalcogenides to quantum dots, and on their use for entanglement, quantum teleportation, entanglement swapping and quantum key distribution.
